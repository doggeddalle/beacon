Beacon is a lightweight, self-updating DLNA / UPnP-AV media server. It streams
the videos, music and photos on your NAS to smart TVs and other DLNA players on
your local network, and keeps its library up to date automatically as files are
added, changed or removed — no manual rescans, no nightly database rebuilds.

Features: real-time library updates, metadata (duration/resolution) and sidecar
subtitles, video thumbnails, and a built-in web admin dashboard.
